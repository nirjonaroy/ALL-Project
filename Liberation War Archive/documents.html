Liberation War Archive

The term Liberation War Archive most prominently refers to the Muktijuddho e-Archive (মুক্তিযুদ্ধ ই-আর্কাইভ), a specialized digital library, archive, and research trust founded in 2007 in Dhaka, Bangladesh. It is dedicated to gathering, preserving, and distributing historical materials, books, and documents related to the 1971 Bangladesh Liberation War and the genocide of the Bengali population.
