Product Information

Proper plant care requires balancing water, light, humidity, and nutrients to mimic aplant's
natural environment.Because different plants have unique needs, understanding these fundamentalcategories
will keep your indoor and outdoor garden thriving.

Ophi Leaf

  • The Knuckle Test: Never water on a strict calendar schedule.Stick your finger 5 centimeters into the soil; if it feels damp, do not water. If it feels completely dry, water the plant thoroughly until water leaks out of the bottom drainage holes.
  • Drainage is Mandatory: Never buy a pot without baseline drainage holes. Trapped standing water at the bottom of a pot cuts off oxygen to the roots, causing deadly root rot
  • Match Plant to Light: Look at your room's natural windows before buying.High-light plants (like succulents) will wither in dark corners, while low-light plants (like snake plants) will scorch under intense, direct sun rays.
